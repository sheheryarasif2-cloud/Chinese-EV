border areas, the imports under this part shall be subject to following conditions, namely.

(iii) | The visitor will purchase goods from any category up to US $50 on

specific category, it will be considered as commercial quantity being liable to
duty/taxes on statutory rates.

system for the goods purchased by the visitor on the prescribed format as
notified by the Board.
