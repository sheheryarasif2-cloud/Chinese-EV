## Q1 — How is SRO 656 applied to an electric two-wheeler?

**Ask it with the specifics in hand, or you will get a general answer.**

> "I'm looking at manufacturing electric two-wheelers and I need to understand the in-house
> facility requirement. SRO 656(I)/2006, Section D, is the motorcycle schedule — but it
> requires an engine assembly line with crank cases, crankshaft and connecting rod, spark plug
> and tappet adjustment, oil filling, and an emission tester at final inspection. **An electric
> motorcycle has none of those.**
>
> So how does EDB verify in-house facilities for an electric two-wheeler? Is there an amended
> schedule, an EV-specific criteria list, or a departmental practice for which items are
> waived?"

**Then the question that actually matters:**

> "Is that written down anywhere I can read before I apply?"

### How to judge the answer

| Answer | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Here is the amended schedule / SRO / office memorandum" | 🟢 **Best case.** Get the reference number and read it. Eligibility becomes assessable. |
| "We assess it case by case" | 🟠 Confirms the discretion finding. **Then ask: what have EV applicants actually been required to install?** Push for specifics. |
| "The same requirements apply" | 🔴 Ask directly whether an engine assembly line and emission tester are genuinely required for a vehicle with no engine. If yes, the assembly route is effectively closed. |
| Vague or deflecting | Escalate to Policy Development Group. |

## Q2 — Does the 200-unit CBU pilot still exist?

> "AIDEP 2021-26 allowed a company investing in EV technology to import **10 CBU units per
> variant at 50% of prevailing customs duty, up to a maximum 200 units per company** across
> scooters, motorcycles, rickshaws and loaders — with manufacturing compulsory within two
> years, recommended by EDB and approved by MoIP.
>
> **AIDEP expired on 30 June 2026. Is that facility still available for two-wheelers?** I know
> the four-wheeler version continued — what is the position for two-wheelers now?"

### Follow up on whichever way it goes

- **If yes:** what is the current legal instrument, what does an application need, and how long
  does approval take?
- **If no:** is anything replacing it, and does the pending AIDEP 2026-31 draft include it?
- **Either way:** does the 30 June 2027 sunset on EV CKD concessions affect it?
