# Pakistani market data — what is safe to use, and what is not

**Recorded 25-Aug-2026**, after checking press-reported figures against PAMA and EDB directly.

## 🔴 PAMA does not count the electric two-wheeler makers

PAMA describes itself as "the principal source of statistical data on the entire automotive
industry of the country" and is licensed by the Ministry of Commerce. Its published statistics
are nevertheless **member-reported**, and its membership (checked 25-Aug-2026 at
`pama.org.pk/members/`) is roughly twenty companies:

- **Motorcycle members:** Atlas Honda, Yamaha Motor Pakistan, United Auto Industries,
  Plum Qingqi, Ravi Automobile, Hinopak
- **Car / commercial:** Pak Suzuki, Indus Motor, Honda Atlas Cars, Hyundai Nishat, Sigma,
  Ghandhara (×2), Master Motor, Dewan Farooque, United Motors
- **Tractors:** Millat, Al-Ghazi · **Other:** Sazgar Engineering, Eiffel Industries

**Of the electric two-wheeler names in circulation — Jolta, Vlektra, MS Jaguar, Road Prince,
Metro, Pakzon, Crown, Okla, ELFA, EcoDost — only Sazgar appears**, and Sazgar is principally a
three-wheeler and car manufacturer.

### What follows from that

🔴 **The "electric two-wheelers are ~8–9% of the market" figure is not defensible, and I should
not have written it.** It divides an electric number sourced from trade press and
MotorCyclesData by a total-market number that is essentially PAMA-member sales — overwhelmingly
combustion, and excluding almost every electric maker. **The numerator and the denominator come
from different populations.** A ratio between two incompatible datasets is not a penetration
rate.

This is the same failure the project's standing rule against averaging mirror statistics exists
to prevent: two figures that look comparable, measured on different bases.

## What each figure is actually worth

| Figure | Value | Basis | Usable? |

| Total motorcycles FY26 | 1.93m units | Press, citing PAMA-type data — member-reported | As a **combustion-market** scale only |
| H1 2026 two-wheelers | 1.1m, +33.8% | Same | Same caveat |
| Electric 2W, 2025 | 112,000 | MotorCyclesData / press | Directionally, **not** as a share |
| Electric 2W, Apr–Jun 2026 | 90,000 | MotorCyclesData / press | Directionally only |

## EDB has no public licence register

The Engineering Development Board site carries the NEV Policy and the AIDEP, but **no published
register of licensed EV manufacturers** could be found. The licence counts in circulation — "31
licences, about 6 actually manufacturing", later "50+ licences" — are **press attributions to
EDB officials, not a document.** They may well be accurate; they are not sourced.

A `pave.gov.pk` portal is referenced and has not yet been examined.

## What to do about it

1. **Obtain PAMA's actual data files.** The monthly and annual series are downloadable from
   `pama.org.pk`; the historical set is partly paid. Only the file itself shows which categories
   are counted.
2. **Find a same-basis electric series.** Registration data — vehicles actually plated — would
   give a single population for both numerator and denominator. That is the honest route to a
   penetration figure.
3. **Get the EDB licence register as a document**, or record the counts permanently as
   press-attributed.

## The rule this earns

⚠️ **Before dividing one figure by another, confirm both count the same population.** A
denominator that excludes most of the numerator's industry produces a plausible, quotable,
wrong number — and it is quotable precisely because it looks reasonable.
