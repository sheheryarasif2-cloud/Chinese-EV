# The market is far bigger than production — correcting a headline claim

**Recorded 30-Aug-2026.**

---

## 🔴 CORRECTION — "5.2× national production" is true but misleading

`state-demand-and-financing.md` and the published artifact both lead with:

> *"The government is subsidising 116,000 machines in a year in which the entire country produced
> 22,404. The programme is 5.2× total national production."*

**Every number in that sentence is accurate. The framing is not.** It compares a subsidy target
against **production**, and invites the reader to conclude the programme dwarfs the market.
**It does not — because Pakistan's e-2W market is already about the size of the programme.**

| Measure | Figure | Source | Period |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Domestic e-2W production** | **22,404** | EDB | **FY2024-25** ⚠️ reported as Jul–Mar, i.e. **9 months** |
| **E-2W units SOLD** | **~112,000** | MotorCyclesData | **calendar 2025** |
| **E-2W units SOLD** | **~90,000** | MotorCyclesData | **April–June 2026 alone** |
| Growth | **+173%** | MotorCyclesData | H1 2026 |
| **Total motorcycle sales** | **1.93 million** | **Arif Habib Limited** | **FY26** — record, prev. 1.85m FY21 |

🔴 **So PAVE's 116,000 is roughly ONE YEAR of current e-2W sales — not five times the market.**
And Q2 2026 alone (~90,000) was already **four times** the annual production figure.

## 🔴 The more important consequence: most e-2W sold here are not made here

**Sales of ~112,000 against production of 22,404 leaves a gap of roughly 90,000 units.**

That gap is either **imported CBUs**, or the EDB production figure is partial — and it is explicitly
a **nine-month** figure, which alone cannot close a 5× gap.

🟢 **If the import share is anything like that large, it materially strengthens the entry case.**
This research has spent days on the CKD/EDB route on the assumption that domestic assembly is the
only viable channel. **A market where roughly four in five electric two-wheelers sold are not
domestically produced has an import channel operating at scale right now.**

⚠️ **But do not bank it yet.** Two measurement problems have to be closed first:
1. **Different periods** — production is FY Jul–Mar; sales are calendar year. They are not
   like-for-like.
2. **Different collectors** — EDB counts licensed manufacturers' output; MotorCyclesData is a
   commercial market-data provider whose Pakistani methodology was **not obtainable** (both its
   pages returned timeout and 403).

🔴 **This is exactly the mismatch that produced the withdrawn "8–9% penetration" figure earlier in
this project — dividing one body's numerator by another body's denominator.** I am not repeating
that mistake: **the gap is flagged as a finding to verify, not stated as an import volume.**

## Penetration — the figure this project withdrew, revisited

Earlier a penetration figure of "~8–9%" was **withdrawn** for dividing press electric numbers by
PAMA combustion-only totals.

**The same arithmetic remains structurally unsafe**, because:
- **1.93m is Arif Habib industry data, and the source article does not mention electric two-wheelers
  at all** — consistent with the finding that industry bodies count members only, excluding almost
  every electric maker.
- So 1.93m is very likely a **combustion denominator**, and the true total market is **1.93m + e-2W**.

**Computed properly on that basis:** 112,000 ÷ (1,930,000 + 112,000) = **~5.5%**.
A third-party post referenced **"4.6%"**, which sits in the same range.

🟢 **So a defensible penetration range is roughly 4.5–5.5%** — materially higher than the ~1% that
production figures alone imply, and materially lower than the withdrawn 8–9%.
⚠️ **Stated as a range, with both inputs named, and still not a citable figure** until the e-2W
sales series can be sourced directly.

## And one genuinely notable claim

**Pakistan is reportedly the 5th largest electric two-wheeler market in the world.**

⚠️ Single-source, from the same provider whose pages could not be opened. **If true it reframes the
whole project** — this is not a nascent market being entered early; it is a top-five global market
already moving at +173%. **If false it is a marketing line.** 🔴 **Verify before it appears in
anything shown to an investor.**
