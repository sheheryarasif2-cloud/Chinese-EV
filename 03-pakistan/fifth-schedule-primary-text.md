# The Fifth Schedule — primary text, and the battery tariff lines

**Recorded 27-Aug-2026.** Source: **`THE FIFTH SCHEDULE TO THE CUSTOMS ACT, 1969 (IV OF 1969)`**,
FBR, 87pp, PDF title *"PAKISTAN CUSTOMS TARIFF"*, **created 5 Aug 2025** — i.e. the **FY2025-26**
edition, published after the Finance Act 2025. Downloaded to `06-sources/`.
Statutory rates from the **Trade Information Portal of Pakistan (TIPP)**, Pakistan Single Window
(Government of Pakistan), read 27-Aug-2026.

🔴 **This replaces FBR's budget summary, which is all the research previously had.**

## 1. The operative provision, as printed

> **Part-V(A) — Import of Electric Vehicles (EV) CBU & their Parts**
> **Under Electric Vehicle Policy 2020**
> **TABLE-II — [For CKD & EV Specific Parts]**
> **Serial 3. Electric motorcycle (PCT code 8711.6040)**

| Imported goods | Customs duty |
|---|---|
| **(i) Following EV specific components for assembly / manufacturing in any kit form (CKD)** | **1%** *(notwithstanding the rate of customs duty as specified in the First Schedule to the Customs Act, 1969)* |
| (ii) Component for Assembly / manufacturing in any kit form (CKD) — **Non-Localized parts** | **15%** |
| (iii) Component for Assembly / manufacturing in any kit form (CKD) — **Localized parts** | **15% plus Additional Customs Duty levied under SRO 693(I)/2006 dated 01.07.2006** |

**The seven components that get 1%, with their PCT codes as printed:**

| | Component | PCT code |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Electric Motor | 8501.3200 |
| (b) | Battery Charger | 8504.4020 |
| (c) | Switches | 8536.5029 |
| (d) | Junction Box | 8536.4910 |
| (e) | Controller | 8542.3100 |
| (f) | Converter | **8454.1000** ⚠️ see §3 |
| **(g)** | **Batteries other than lead acid** | **85.07** |

**Condition, column (5), verbatim in substance:** the concession is admissible **to manufacturers
of electric motorcycle for a period of five years from 1st July, 2020, subject to certification
and quota determination by the Engineering Development Board (EDB).**

Serials (ii) and (iii) are both conditioned on **Para-2 of SRO 656(I)/2006 dated 22.06.2006** —
the instrument already documented in `edb-certification.md` as containing **no EV provisions at
all.** That link is now confirmed from the primary text, not inferred.

## 2. 🔴 Three corrections to earlier work in this project

**1. Non-localised CKD parts are 15%, not "10–15%."** Every prior file in this workspace carried
"10–15%" from FBR's budget summary. **The schedule says 15% flat** for non-localised, and 15% +
ACD for localised. **Localised parts are dearer than non-localised**, which is the whole point —
it penalises importing what Pakistan already makes.

**2. This is the EV Policy 2020 regime, not the NEV Policy 2025-30.** The heading says so.
The **NEV Policy 2025-30 has no corresponding Fifth Schedule table yet.** The concession an
assembler would actually claim in 2026 is a 2020-policy instrument.

**3. 🔴 DO NOT read the expiry off this text.** As printed it says *"five years from 1st July,
2020"* — which reads as **30 June 2025**, already past. But the **Finance Act 2026 extended the
1% EV CKD concession to 30 June 2027** (FBR Salient Features Budget 2026-27; corroborated by
Profit/Pakistan Today, 12-Jun-2026). Table-I's four-wheeler entries similarly print
*"till 30th June 2026"* while the extension moved them too.

⚠️ **This is the exact trap this project fell into once already** (recorded in `CLAUDE.md`: a
policy's stated end date is not the law in force; the annual finance act overtakes it).
**The FY2025-26 schedule text and the operative date now differ. Neither the printed text nor a
press summary settles it — only the FY2026-27 schedule or an SRO does.**
`[PLACEHOLDER: FY2026-27 Fifth Schedule]` — **not found on fbr.gov.pk.** The Budget 2026-27
folder returns 404 for the customs schedules; only Salient Features is posted.

## 3. ⚠️ A drafting defect in the schedule itself

Serial 3 gives **Converter = PCT 8454.1000.**

**8454 is "Converters, ladles, ingot moulds and casting machines, of a kind used in metallurgy or
in metal foundries."** 8454.10 is the metallurgical converter — a steel-making vessel.

**Serial 1 (electric auto rickshaw) gives Converter = 8502.4000** — *electric rotary converters*,
which is the sensible code.

So the schedule assigns a **foundry** code to an electric motorcycle's converter, and a different
code for the same component on an auto rickshaw. **An importer declaring a DC-DC converter under
8454.1000 is declaring a steel converter.** Whether Customs accepts the schedule's own code or
reclassifies is not a question this research can answer — **it is a question for a clearing agent
before any goods ship**, and it is exactly the kind of paperwork error that ended companies in
India (`india-fame-shakeout.md`).

## 4. 🟢 The battery HS code — answered, and it is three answers

### Route A — battery pack as an EV CKD component: **1%**
**"Batteries other than lead acid (PCT code 85.07)"**, Part-V(A) Table-II serial 3(i)(g).

🔴 **Lead-acid is expressly excluded.** The concession is written *"other than lead acid"* in all
three two/three-wheeler serials. **Pakistan's tariff will not subsidise the cheap chemistry.**
Requires being an **EDB-certified electric motorcycle manufacturer** with quota determination.

### Route B — lithium cells as a battery maker: **0%**
Fifth Schedule **Part-I, serial 143**:

> **143 · Lithium Ion Cells · 8507.6000 · 0% ·** *"If imported by manufacturers registered under
> the Sales Tax Act 1990, of lithium-ion batteries subject to quota determination by IOCO."*

### Route C — a full pack BOM as a battery assembler: **0%**
Fifth Schedule Part-I, **"(d) Parts of Lithium Batteries — 0%"**, *"If imported by the local
assemblers or manufacturers of Lithium Batteries registered under the Sales Tax Act, 1990,
subject to quota determination by IOCO"*:

| Part | PCT |
|---|---|
| Cells | 8507.9000 |
| Copper bar (cell-to-cell connection) | 7407.1010 |
| BMS (level 1) electronic card | 8507.9000 |
| Casing | 8507.9000 |
| Harness set (cell monitoring wires with tags) | 8544.4290 |
| Output terminal with screws | 8536.9090 |
| Power cables (battery internal) | 8544.4290 |
| DC fan | 8414.5990 |
| DC breaker | 8536.2010 / 8536.2020 / 8536.2090 |
| Packing screws | 7318.1590 |
| Terminal covers | 3926.9099 |
| Acrylic sheet (short-circuit safety sheet) | 3920.5900 / 3921.9090 |
| Other accessories (temp sensors, connectors, handles) | 8536.9090 / 9031.8000 / 8507.9000 |

🔴 **This is the most strategically interesting finding in the file.** Route C needs **no EDB
vehicle certification at all** — only Sales Tax registration as a lithium battery assembler plus
an IOCO quota. **The state has built a cheaper, lower-barrier on-ramp into the battery than into
the vehicle**, and `battery-import-cost.md` shows the battery is **53% of FOB cost**.

## 5. What it costs with no concession — statutory rates <span>(TIPP, Govt of Pakistan)</span>

### 8507.6000 — Lithium-ion *(commodity code 850760001000)*

| Duty | Rate | Valid from | Valid to |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **CD** Statutory Customs Duty | **10%** | 01-07-2025 | 31-12-2040 |
| **RD** Statutory Regulatory Duty | **2%** | **01-07-2026** | **30-06-2027** |
| **ST (VAT)** Sales Tax | **18%** | 01-07-2013 | 31-12-2040 |
| **IT** Income Tax (withholding) | **11%** | 01-07-2020 | 31-12-2040 |

⚠️ **That 12-digit code reads "Secondary lithium cells and batteries for portable application"** —
a 72V traction pack is arguably **not** portable-application, and another 12-digit sub-code may
govern. **The heading-level rates should hold; the sub-code must be confirmed before filing.**

### 8711.6040 — Electric motorcycles (CBU)

| Duty | Rate | Valid from |
|---|---:|---|
| **CD** Statutory Customs Duty | **30%** | **01-07-2026** |
| **ACD** Additional Customs Duty | **4%** | **01-07-2026** |
| **ST (VAT)** Sales Tax | **18%** | 01-07-2020 |
| **IT** Income Tax | **12%** | 01-07-2023 |

*(8711.6060, the 3-wheeler loader, carries the identical CD 30% / ACD 4% from the same date.)*

**Provincial cess on top, by port of clearance:** **Sindh 1.80–1.85%** (Karachi) · Punjab 0.90% ·
KPK 2% · Balochistan 1.10–1.50%.

### Two more things TIPP records that matter

🔴 **A PSQCA Release Certificate is a listed Certificate Requirement on 8507.6000** — Pakistan
Standards and Quality Control Authority Act 1996. Samples may be lab-tested and **"otherwise the
shipment is destroyed."** This partially closes the PSQCA gap flagged in `edb-certification.md`
— **for the battery. The vehicle's own type-approval path is still unconfirmed.**

⚠️ **The Pak-China FTA rate on 8507.6000 was 4.58% and expired 31-12-2023.** Do not assume an
FTA preference on Chinese-origin batteries.

⚠️ **A data inconsistency in TIPP itself:** the ACD exemption for 2-3 wheeler EV CBUs under
**SRO 929(I)/2024 sr.3(xi)** is described as running *"till 30th June, 2025"* while its
`valid_to` field shows **31-12-2040**. **The description and the field disagree. Do not rely on
either without checking the SRO.**

## 6. The gap between qualifying and not

Rates only — **no total is stated here, because sales tax and withholding apply to
duty-inclusive value and the exact stacking order is a clearing-agent question, not a desk one.**

| Route | Customs duty on the battery |
|---|---|
| **Route B/C** — registered lithium battery maker, IOCO quota | **0%** |
| **Route A** — EDB-certified e-motorcycle maker, CKD | **1%** |
| **No qualification** — plain commercial import | **10% CD + 2% RD**, then 18% ST + 11% IT |

| Route | Customs duty on the vehicle |
|---|---|
| **CKD**, EDB-certified, non-localised parts | **15%** |
| **CBU**, no concession | **30% CD + 4% ACD**, then 18% ST + 12% IT |

🟢 **The policy's intent is now visible in the numbers rather than inferred: CBU import is
penalised, CKD assembly is favoured, and local battery manufacture is favoured most of all.**

⚠️ **But note what that means for the thesis.** The 1% headline rate is not the prize — **the
prize is not paying 30% + 4% + 18% + 12% on a CBU.** And the gate to that prize is EDB
certification against **SRO 656(I)/2006, an instrument with no EV provisions**, which the primary
text has now confirmed is the operative condition. **K7 and the EDB question are the same
question.**
