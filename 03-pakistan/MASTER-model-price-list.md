**Recorded 27-Aug-2026.** Source: PakWheels brand pages, pulled directly per brand.
**Evidence grade: retailer/aggregator listing.** Prices are PKR, new, and move.

**~90 models across 13 brands.** This is as complete as Pakistan's documented electric

## 🔴 The headline: Atlas Honda's electric entry is NOT a fleet product

**Honda ICON e: — PKR 419,900**

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Motor | 1500 W · 85 Nm |
| Top speed | 55 km/h |
| **Battery** | **Li-ion 1.58 kWh — FIXED, non-swappable** |
| **Range** | **65 km** |
| **Charging** | **6.0 hours · proprietary charger · NO fast charge** |
| Weight | 86 kg dry |
| Wheels | 12″ · 3.5-12 front, 4-10 rear |
| Other | Regenerative braking · Black / Red / White |

**Read what this actually is.** At PKR 419,900 it is **2.6× a CD 70** and **1.7× a Yadea T5** —
while offering **less range than the T5 (65 km vs 75–85 km)** and far less than the T5L's 80–140 km.

🟢 **And it is structurally useless for fleet work.** A rider covering 80–128 km a day cannot
use a **65 km machine with a fixed battery, a proprietary charger and a six-hour recharge.**
There is no swap, no fast charge, and no second pack.

**The incumbent has taken a premium consumer position, not a fleet one.** The gap this project
identified is not closing — it is confirmed, by the only company that could have closed it.

## 🔴 What this list changes

1. **The entry point is far lower than previously recorded.** Evee Flipper **PKR 97,000** and
   REVOO A04 **PKR 100,000** sit *well below* the CD 70's 159,900 — not at parity.
   **Earlier notes putting the entry tier at PKR 145,000–210,000 were wrong and are superseded.**
2. **The market is enormously crowded at 150,000–250,000** — roughly 35 models from 10 brands.
   **Nothing is differentiated on price. Nothing.**
3. **Chemistry is the real price ladder, and it is now measurable.** MS Jaguar's Bolt is 174,000
   in standard trim and **245,000 in LFP** — the same machine, +PKR 71,000 for lithium. REVOO's
   A11 shows +42,000, A12 +62,000. **That premium is the single most important number for a fleet
   buyer**, given lead-acid dies in 8–13 months at fleet duty cycles.
4. **Atlas Honda priced itself out of the volume market and out of fleet simultaneously.**
   419,900 with 65 km and a fixed pack is a statement product.
5. **Yadea spans 174,000 to 1,400,000** — the only brand credibly present at both ends.
