# Sindh does have an EV programme — I framed this wrongly

**Recorded 29-Aug-2026.**

---

## 🔴 CORRECTION — "Karachi has almost nothing" was too strong

`swapping-and-the-punjab-concentration.md` states that every significant EV initiative is in Punjab
and that **"Sindh offers none of that."** That is **true only of consumer tax concessions and
provincial e-bike subsidy schemes.** It is **wrong as a statement about Sindh's EV activity.**

**Sindh Budget 2026-27 allocates PKR 39.5 billion to transport and communications**, and the
programme it funds is substantial:

| Sindh initiative | Detail |
|---|---|
| **EV taxi service** | Launching **September 2026** |
| **Electric buses** | **1,000 in phases**; **500 already approved** by the Policy Board |
| Double-decker buses | **50 to Karachi, October 2026** |
| 🔴 **Pink EV scooters for women** | **A two-wheeler programme, in Sindh** |
| People's Bus Service, pink taxis | Existing services being extended |
| 🟢 **Charging infrastructure** | **Explicitly included in the allocation** |

Reviewed at a Transport and Mass Transit Department meeting chaired by Senior Minister
**Sharjeel Inam Memon**, 4 August 2026. Sindh was also **the first province to introduce electric
buses in Pakistan.**

### What the corrected picture actually looks like

**Sindh's EV strategy is public-transport-led and fleet-led. Punjab's is consumer-subsidy-led.**
They are different strategies, not presence versus absence.

| | Punjab | Sindh |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer e-bike subsidy | ✅ 100,000 units, PKR 70,000 each | ❌ None found |
| Provincial tax concession | ✅ 95% registration + MV tax | ❌ None published |
| Public EV fleet | Less prominent | 🟢 **1,000 e-buses, EV taxis, 50 double-deckers** |
| **Two-wheeler programme** | Student e-bikes | 🟢 **Pink EV scooters for women** |
| Charging infrastructure | Donor + private (Zyp, MAF) | 🟢 **Publicly funded in the budget** |

🟢 **This improves the Karachi case rather than weakening it.** A province funding EV taxis, 1,000
e-buses, women's e-scooters and charging infrastructure is **building demand and grid capability in
the target city** — even though it is not handing consumers a subsidy.

⚠️ **But the original point survives in narrower form:** an entrant selling to *private buyers or
delivery riders* in Karachi still gets **no provincial subsidy and no tax concession**, while the
same entrant in Lahore would. **The consumer-facing gap is real. The "nothing is happening"
framing was not.**

🔴 **And "pink EV scooters for women" is a direct government two-wheeler procurement in Sindh.**
Whether that is tendered, and to whom, is **worth asking** — it is the Sindh analogue of the EDB
500-unit batch structure. `[PLACEHOLDER: procurement route for Sindh pink EV scooters]`

## PSQCA — 172 standards exist, but no lithium testing laboratory

Earlier files treated PSQCA certification as a **paper gate of unknown content**. It has content:

> **PSQCA has established 172 standards for electric motorcycles.**

🔴 **But it cannot fully test to them.** Reporting from the National Assembly panel is explicit:
**PSQCA "needs to strengthen its technical capacity and establish dedicated lithium battery testing
laboratories."**

**So the position is: standards exist on paper; the capability to verify lithium packs against them
does not.** That is consistent with the grey-market finding already recorded — a certificate that
cannot be tested against is a certificate that can be obtained by anyone.

### The four-way regulatory split, confirmed

| Function | Body | Ministry |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing licences | **EDB** | Industries & Production |
| Quality standards for e-motorcycles **and batteries** | **PSQCA** | Science & Technology |
| Charging infrastructure | **NEECA** | Energy |
| Road regulation, registration | **Provincial authorities** | — |

⚠️ **Four bodies across three federal ministries plus four provinces**, with **no dedicated
e-motorcycle regulator** — which is what the NA panel flagged.
