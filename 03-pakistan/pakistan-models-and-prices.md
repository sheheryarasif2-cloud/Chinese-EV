# Pakistan — electric two-wheeler companies, models and prices

**Recorded 26-Aug-2026.** Evidence grade: **press and retailer listings** unless marked.
Prices are retail, PKR, and move — treat as a snapshot, not a schedule.

⚠️ **Coverage note.** This is the substantially complete field of *documented* Pakistani
electric two-wheeler brands. Where a brand's model line is known but individual prices are not
published, the models are listed and the price marked `—`. Nothing has been estimated.

---

## The field at a glance

| Segment | PKR | Who plays there |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry / 70cc-replacement** | 145,000 – 210,000 | Jolta, Pakzon, EcoDost, Okla Orbit, ELFA EV-1 |
| **Mid / commuter** | 220,000 – 290,000 | Yadea T5 & T5L, MS Jaguar, Metro, ELFA EV-125, Pakzon PE-100L |
| **Premium / performance** | 370,000 – 819,000 | Vlektra, Okla OMAX & OKG |

**Benchmark to beat: Honda CD 70 at PKR 159,900.** Only the entry tier is price-competitive
with it on sticker; everything above wins on running cost instead.

## What this table says about entry strategy

1. 🔴 **The entry tier sits at PKR 145,000–210,000 — at or below the CD 70's PKR 159,900.**
   The project has been anchored on the Yadea T5 at PKR 245,000, which is *mid-market*, not the
   volume segment. Consistent with the Punjab scheme implying PKR 63,000–87,000 financed bikes
   (`05-numbers/rider-affordability.md`), **the cheap end is where the volume is.**
2. **Price alone is a crowded fight.** At least five brands sit within PKR 15,000 of each other
   at the entry point. Nothing differentiates on sticker.
3. **Specification quality is wildly uneven** — dry-gel 48V packs at one end, Samsung cells with
   ABS and a five-year warranty at the other, at 5.5× the price. **Battery chemistry and warranty
   are where the real differences hide**, and they are exactly what a fleet buyer cares about.
4. **Almost no brand publishes service-network data.** Given that service is what destroyed
   India's largest player, that silence is itself informative.
