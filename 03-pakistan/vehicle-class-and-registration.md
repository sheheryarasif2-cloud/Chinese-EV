# Is there a low-speed class? — the vehicle definition, from primary law

**Recorded 27-Aug-2026.** Source: **The Provincial Motor Vehicles Ordinance, 1965 (W.P. Ord. XIX
of 1965)**, full text, read directly. Archived as `06-sources/motor-vehicles-ordinance-1965.pdf`.

**The question:** `MASTER-india-model-price-list.md` found that India's entry tier — Lectrix SX25
at ₹54,999, Ampere Reo at ₹71,999 — sits in a **25 kmph low-speed class needing no licence or
registration**, and flagged that a Pakistani equivalent "would open a price tier below
PKR 97,000." **This settles whether one exists.**

---

## 🔴 The answer: no such class exists, and the law leaves no room to read one in

### "motor vehicle" — section 2, verbatim

> *"**motor vehicle**" means any mechanically propelled vehicle adapted for use upon roads
> **whether the powers of propulsion is transmitted thereto from an external or internal source**,
> and includes a chassis to which a body has not been attached or a tractor and a trailer; but
> does not include a vehicle running upon fixed rails or used solely upon the premises of the
> owner.*

### "motor cycle" — section 2, verbatim

> *"**motor cycle**" means a motor vehicle other than an invalid carriage, **with less than four
> wheels**, the unladen weight of which, inclusive of any side-car attached to the vehicle,
> **does not exceed 900 pounds avoirdupois**.*

### What those two definitions do and do not contain

**The only tests are:** mechanically propelled · adapted for road use · fewer than four wheels ·
**unladen weight ≤ 900 lb (408 kg)**.

🔴 **There is no wattage, no kW, no cc, no top-speed and no power threshold anywhere in either
definition.** Searched the whole Ordinance for `watt`, `kilowatt`, `cc`, `cubic centimetre` and
`horse power`: the only hits are **"Horse power" as a blank field on the registration form** and a
**cc-banded fee table** (800cc / 801–1000 / 1001–1300 / 1301–2000 / above 2000) which sets fees,
not eligibility.

🟢 **And note the drafting: "whether the powers of propulsion is transmitted thereto from an
external or internal source."** A 1965 statute that already contemplates non-combustion
propulsion. **Electric vehicles were never outside this Ordinance and never needed an amendment
to be brought inside it.**

**So every electric two-wheeler sold in Pakistan is a "motor cycle" in law.** The heaviest machine
in the catalogues — the Honda ICON e: at 86 kg dry — is **less than a quarter** of the 408 kg
ceiling. Nothing on the market comes close to falling out of the definition.

## Registration is unconditional — section 23(1), verbatim

> *"**No person shall drive any motor vehicle and no owner of a motor vehicle shall cause or
> permit the vehicle to be driven in any place unless the vehicle is registered** in accordance
> with this Chapter and the vehicle carries a registration mark displayed in the prescribed
> manner."*

The exemptions that exist in the Chapter are narrow and unrelated: vehicles **in the possession of
dealers**, **road-rollers and graders**, driving **to the place of registration**, and light goods
vehicles from section 39 only. **There is no exemption keyed to power, speed or vehicle size.**

## ⚠️ This CORRECTS a widely repeated claim

Multiple Pakistani EV vendor pages state that *"low-speed e-bikes (below 250W) may not require
registration, depending on local laws."*

**That has no basis in the Ordinance.** The definitions carry no power threshold and section 23 is
unconditional. The claim appears to be **Indian rules (250W / 25 kmph) restated for a Pakistani
audience.**

🔴 **Treat it as wrong until someone produces the Pakistani instrument that says otherwise.**
Selling on the promise of "no registration needed" would be selling a legal exposure to the buyer.

## What it means for the entry decision

1. 🔴 **The sub-PKR-97,000 tier cannot be unlocked by regulatory arbitrage.** India's entry price
   point exists because India *created* a class beneath registration. **Pakistan would have to
   amend provincial primary legislation — in four provinces separately**, since the Ordinance is
   provincial. That is not a business timeline.

2. 🟢 **But the same fact removes an objection to the fleet thesis.** A delivery rider's machine
   was always going to be registered, licensed and plated. **The fleet model never depended on an
   exemption**, so nothing here weakens it. It only closes off a *different* strategy.

3. ⚠️ **Registration is a per-unit operating cost and a per-unit friction**, not a one-off.
   Provincial Excise & Taxation registration, plate, and 3–7 working days per vehicle
   `[VERIFY: fee per e-bike by province]`. **For a 200-machine fleet that is 200 registrations** —
   an administrative load the fleet model has not costed.

4. 🟢 **It also means every machine is traceable**, which is what makes a rental fleet financeable
   at all: a registered asset can be recovered, insured and pledged. **A hypothetical unregistered
   class would have been worse for this business, not better.**

## 🔴 Sindh's fee schedule has no line an electric bike can be assessed on

**Source:** Sindh Excise, Taxation & Narcotics Control Department, published motor vehicle
registration fee schedule, read 27-Aug-2026.

| Category | Registration fee |
|---|---|
| **Motorcycles / scooters, not more than 149 cc** | **1% of vehicle value** |
| **Motorcycles / scooters, 150 cc and above** | **2% of vehicle value** |
| Cars up to 1000 cc | 1% |
| Tractor for agriculture | Rs. 2,000 flat |

🔴 **The entire schedule is banded by engine capacity, and an electric motorcycle has none.**
There is **no EV category, no kW band and no flat rate** for a vehicle with zero cc.

⚠️ **But the department clearly knows EVs exist** — the *penalty* section expressly names
**"EV Scooters"**, charging **Rs. 5,000 where registration exceeds 30 days.** So Sindh imposes a
late-registration penalty on a vehicle class for which it publishes no registration fee.

🟢 **That penalty line is independent corroboration of the Ordinance finding above:** registration
of an electric scooter is compulsory in Sindh, on the department's own document.

**The practical consequence, stated honestly:** at 1% of value a PKR 150,000 machine registers for
**PKR 1,500**; at 2%, **PKR 3,000**. Across a 200-machine fleet that is **PKR 300,000–600,000**.
The band is presumably the ≤149cc one — **but that is an inference, not a published rule, and the
uncertainty is a clean 2×.** `[PLACEHOLDER: which band Sindh Excise actually applies to an EV]`
