### 🟢 But it validates the fleet arithmetic, precisely

`fleet-model.md` modelled the operator collecting **PKR 300–450/day**, i.e. **PKR 7,800–11,700 a
month**, out of a gross fuel-to-electricity headroom of **PKR 16,105/month**.

**ELFA and Bykea claim the rider saves up to PKR 8,000/month** after paying the instalment.
**16,105 − 8,000 ≈ 8,100 to the financier** — which lands almost exactly on the modelled
**PKR 300–350/day** band.

🟢 **An independent commercial offer, built by people with real riders, prices at the number this
model derived from first principles.** That is the strongest validation the fleet economics have had
— and it did not come from a source that had seen the model.

## 🔴 The bigger pattern: this is a Punjab phenomenon

Every significant EV two-wheeler initiative found is in Punjab or Islamabad. **Karachi — the largest
city, the biggest delivery market, and the port every import lands at — has almost nothing.**

| Initiative | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| CM e-Bike Scheme, 100,000 units | **Punjab** | PKR 70,000 subsidy · PKR 2,100/month |
| foodpanda + Wasl EV rider fleet | **Lahore / Punjab** | NBFC financing for riders |
| **Zyp Technologies** — BaaS network | **Lahore** | **60+ swap stations, 1,000 bikes** |
| ezBike / **ezSwap** | **Islamabad + Rawalpindi** | First swappable-battery scooter, "Electron" |
| First **public** e-bike swap station | **Faisalabad** | 2 cabinets, 5 bikes → **50 stations by Dec 2026** |
| **Mitigation Action Facility** EUR 8.9m | **Faisalabad, Lahore, Multan** | **137 swap stations, 9,614 e-3W** |
| Provincial tax treatment | Balochistan / Punjab / KPK have concessions | 🔴 **Sindh publishes none** |
| **ELFA + Bykea** | 🟢 **Karachi** | The one Karachi-anchored initiative found |
| Karachi lithium-battery pilot | Karachi | ⚠️ *"planned for Q3 2026"* — nothing operational found |

**Two readings, and both deserve weight:**

🟢 **The opportunity reading.** The largest city is underserved, and it is where the port is. Every
imported kit and battery clears at Karachi anyway; the infrastructure gap sits on top of the
logistics advantage.

🔴 **The warning reading.** Punjab was not chosen at random. It has a provincial subsidy, a
provincial bank administering it, a provincial government co-launching with foodpanda, and
international climate finance targeting three of its cities. **Sindh offers none of that.** The
ecosystem went where the support was — and an entrant in Karachi would be operating without any of
it.

### The public and donor layer

**Faisalabad** launched Pakistan's **first public-sector** e-bike swapping pilot: **2 cabinets and
5 motorcycles**, with a stated plan of **50 stations by December 2026**, accessed through an
**E-Swap** app with digital subscription payment. ⚠️ **Note the gap between 2 cabinets today and 50
stations in four months** — treat the target as an intention.

**Mitigation Action Facility — "Swappable Battery-based Network for Promoting Electric Vehicles in
Pakistan":**

| | |
|---|---|
| Funding | **EUR 8.9 million** — EUR 3m demonstration grants + **EUR 3m first-loss guarantees to leverage EUR 18.6m of commercial lending** |
| Implementer | **LUMS** |
| Banking partners | **Habib Bank Limited** and **Meezan Bank** |
| Targets | **9,614 swappable electric three-wheelers · 137 battery swap stations · 12.3 MWp solar PV** |
| Cities | **Faisalabad, Lahore, Multan** |
| Timeline | Pilot 09/2024–11/2025 · **implementation 08/2026–07/2031** |

🟢 **The first-loss guarantee structure is worth understanding even as a non-participant.** EUR 3m
of guarantees unlocking EUR 18.6m of bank lending is a **6.2× leverage ratio** — and it names
**HBL and Meezan** as the lenders willing to fund EV assets against it. **That is a route to K4
(can an LC / can finance be raised) that this research had not considered.**

⚠️ **But note it is THREE-wheelers**, not two, and **Punjab only.**

## 🔴 Zyp's plan against Zyp's own website

**Announced July 2024:** *"over 60 battery swap stations and 1,000 ZUM 2000 motorcycles in the next
12 months."*

**Zyp's own locations page, read 28-Aug-2026 — 25 months later — lists 10 sites, all in Lahore:**

> Zyp Experience Center · TCS Gulberg · TCS Davis Road · Tricon Pvt Ltd · Chughtai Lab 7 Jail Road ·
> Chughtai Lab Bhatta Chowk · Apni Dukaan State Life · METRO Cash & Carry Thokar · The Vertical ·
> Zyp Technologies Factory

🔴 **Two of those are Zyp's own premises.** So **eight third-party host sites against a 60-station
plan — roughly 17% — twenty-five months after the raise, not twelve.**

**Read carefully, because this is evidence about the business model, not a criticism of a company:**

1. 🔴 **A funded, manufacturing, well-backed operator with its own factory has taken 25 months to
   reach 10 swap points in one city.** Any plan in this workspace that assumes a network can be
   stood up quickly should be re-read against that.
2. 🟢 **The host pattern is the interesting part.** TCS (courier depots), Chughtai Lab (diagnostic
   clinics), METRO (wholesale retail), Apni Dukaan. **Zyp is placing cabinets inside other
   companies' existing footprints** — not building sites. That is a capital-light route worth
   copying, and it explains why hardware cost is not the binding constraint.
3. ⚠️ **The arithmetic says funding was probably not the limit.** 60 cabinets at ~US$3,800 is
   ~US$228,000; even with packs and 1,000 bikes the plan plausibly fits inside US$1.5m.
   **So the constraint is more likely siting, permissions, grid connections and demand density than
   money** — which is exactly the kind of thing a desk cannot verify. `[VERIFY with Zyp]`

## Residual value — the fleet model's exit, and it is weak

| | |
|---|---|
| Used e-bikes listed on PakWheels | **PKR 19,000 → 80,000+** |
| General e-bike depreciation | **15–25% per year**, most of it in year one |
| Retained value at three years | **50–60%** |
| Market condition | *"still evolving and not as strong as that for traditional bikes. **Depreciation can be high due to concerns about battery life**"* |

🔴 **This matters directly to the fleet model.** `fleet-model.md` locks capital for **13–20 months
per machine** and assumes recovery at the end. **If a used e-bike sells for PKR 19,000–80,000 and
buyers discount hard for battery uncertainty, the exit value is thin and the asset is effectively
consumed.**

🟢 **It also argues for the swap/BaaS structure over outright sale.** If the operator retains the
battery — the component buyers distrust — the vehicle's residual is less battery-dependent, and the
pack keeps earning after the bike is sold. **That is the strongest argument yet for owning the
battery rather than selling it with the machine**, and it is the same logic Yadea follows at
28.4% of revenue.

⚠️ **Grade honestly:** the depreciation percentages are **generic e-bike industry figures, not
Pakistani data**, and the PKR 19,000–80,000 band is a listings range, not a matched-model
comparison. **No Pakistani residual-value series exists.** `[PLACEHOLDER]`
