folder, none of them to be acted on without re-checking:

- **The question changed shape.** It is no longer *"is there a business here"* — it is
  **"can PKR 50m run it."** A named lender (NCGCL, in Dawn, 29-Aug) puts working capital at
  **PKR 200m per 1,000 units/month**, and a named operating OEM says it cannot fund its next order.
  **The binding constraint moved from regulation to the cash conversion cycle.**
- **The fleet beachhead was refuted, then rebuilt on one condition.** foodpanda: ~40,000 riders,
  ~150 electric — **0.375%**, and the reason is charging time, not price. **Fleet survives only as
  fleet-plus-swapping**, and no deployed delivery-fleet swap network exists in Pakistan.
- **Four independent arguments converge on owning the battery rather than selling it** — warranty
  exposure, residual value, grey-market quality signalling, and a ~50% value loss at the showroom
  door. **The most robust structural conclusion the project has.**
- **Karachi is the harder market, and that is where the owner is** — ground clearance against road
  surface, apartment living against charging access, and the whole policy/swap ecosystem in Punjab.
- **The regulatory work is done to primary-source depth** — Fifth Schedule as substituted by the
  Finance Act 2026, four battery routes, the 30-Jun-2027 sunset, the customs valuation floor.
  🔴 **Nearly every remaining open item is a phone call, not a search.**

## The decision

The whole project is a go/no-go on entering the EV business. Two artefacts hold that, and
both live in the project folder rather than in memory, because they are documents to be
worked on rather than facts to be recalled:

- `01-question/kill-criteria.md` — 11 criteria in four tiers, written before the research so
  they cannot bend to the answer. **K1 (PKR 50m), K2 (30 hrs/week), K5 (10 units' margin) and K8
  (PKR 7.5m) are set; K9 cannot be set until K6 is modelled.**
  🔴 **Its decision log is frozen at 25-Aug-2026 and has four entries.** Nine days of research since
  then — including the working-capital finding that directly challenges the logged *"K1: capital
  does not bind"* — **have never been logged against a criterion.** The governance device the
  project built to stop itself talking past its own gates is the one document not being maintained.
- `ROADMAP.md` — **still not created**, as of 04-Sep-2026. It is named in `CLAUDE.md` as *the*
  living deliverable that everything else feeds.
