# India — master model and price list

**Recorded 27-Aug-2026.** Source: BikeWale electric scooter and electric bike listings, extracted
directly. **Prices are INR, average ex-showroom, before subsidy.**

**36 current models + 8 confirmed upcoming**, with top speed, range, kerb weight and battery
capacity for nearly all — **better specification coverage than the Pakistani list**, because
Indian aggregators publish kWh and range as standard.

**Subsidy in force:** PM E-DRIVE — ₹2,500/kWh capped at ₹5,000/vehicle, to 31 July 2026, no
confirmed successor.

## 🔴 What India's list shows that Pakistan's does not

### 1. Battery capacity is published as standard — and it is 2–3× Pakistan's

Indian mainstream scooters carry **2.5–5.3 kWh**. The Yadea T5, Pakistan's volume import, carries
**1.8 kWh**; Honda's ICON e: in Pakistan carries **1.58 kWh**.

**Indian buyers are getting roughly double the battery for a comparable position in their market.**
That is the single clearest structural difference between the two markets, and it explains the
range gap: **India's mainstream does 120–212 km; Pakistan's does 65–90 km.**

### 2. Range at the mainstream price point is not close

| | Mainstream range |
|---|---|
| **India** (₹1.0–1.3 lakh) | **102–212 km** |
| **Pakistan** (PKR 200–290k) | **65–90 km** |

**Honda sells a 102 km Activa e in India and a 65 km ICON e: in Pakistan.** Same manufacturer,
same segment, materially different product.

### 3. The performance tier is real in India and a curiosity in Pakistan

**OLA Roadster Pro: 194 kmph, 579 km, 16 kWh — ₹1,99,999.** Ultraviolette X47 at 10.3 kWh.
Royal Enfield has entered with the Flying Flea.

Pakistan's equivalent tier is the Okla OKG at PKR 819,000 and Yadea Keeness at PKR 1,400,000 —
**priced far higher relative to their market, with no comparable specification disclosure.**

### 4. Legacy manufacturers now occupy the mainstream, exactly as the shakeout predicted

TVS, Bajaj, Hero (VIDA), Honda, Suzuki, Yamaha, Royal Enfield and Kinetic are all present at
₹90,000–1,90,000. **Ola's cheapest is ₹89,999 and it is losing share anyway** — the clearest
possible confirmation that price was never the deciding variable.

### 5. India's entry point is genuinely low

**Lectrix SX25 at ₹54,999** and **Ampere Reo at ₹71,999** — both 25 kmph low-speed models needing
no licence or registration in India. **Pakistan has no equivalent regulatory category**, which is
worth checking: a low-speed class would open a price point beneath everything currently sold.

## Coverage boundary — stated plainly

This is **BikeWale's full current listing**, which is the most complete public Indian source.
It is **not every SKU in India**: variant-level trims, regional brands (Okinawa, PURE EV, Komaki,
Okaya, BattRE, Bounce, Hero Electric's remnants) and the informal low-speed segment are not
enumerated here. Those brands are named in `india-models-and-prices.md`.

**A truly exhaustive Indian SKU list does not exist in any single public source** — variant trims
alone would multiply this table several times over.
