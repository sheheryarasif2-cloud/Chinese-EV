# India — electric two-wheeler companies, models and prices

**Recorded 26-Aug-2026.** Prices are **INR, ex-showroom, before subsidy**. Evidence grade:
**press and aggregator listings**.

⚠️ **Coverage note.** India has dozens of brands and hundreds of variants. This covers the
**significant field** — everyone with meaningful share, plus the notable specialists. It is not
every SKU, and brands listed without prices are those whose model-level pricing was not obtained.

Read alongside `04-comparables/india-fame-shakeout.md`, which explains why this leaderboard looks
nothing like it did in 2022.

## What India's price structure tells Pakistan

1. 🔴 **The mainstream sits at ₹75,000 – ₹1.25 lakh** — a genuinely mass-market band, achieved
   with a subsidy now cut to ₹5,000 a vehicle. **Volume did not require a large subsidy; it
   required credible product and service.**
2. **Legacy manufacturers cluster tightly** — TVS ₹95k–1.23 lakh, Bajaj ₹91k–1.56 lakh, Hero
   ₹74k–1.20 lakh. They compete on distribution and trust, not price.
3. **The pure-play that priced most aggressively lost.** Ola's S1 X at ₹84,999 undercut almost
   everyone and its share still fell from over 50% to 6.8%.
4. **Performance is a real but tiny niche** — Ultraviolette at ₹3.09 lakh is four times the
   mainstream. Pakistan's Okla OKG at PKR 819,000 occupies the same odd corner.
5. 🔴 **The structural warning:** India's leaderboard was rebuilt entirely in under four years,
   and rebuilt in favour of incumbents with service networks. **Pakistan's incumbent has already
   started.**
