# Chinese electric two-wheeler manufacturers

**Recorded 25-Aug-2026.** Source: China Chamber of Commerce for the Motorcycle Industry, 2025
figures, via trade press. **Evidence grade: association statistics, press-relayed** — not read
from the association's own publication. Upgrade this before the numbers become load-bearing.

## Scope warning, before any number is used

These are **licence-plated electric motorcycles** — a specific registered-vehicle segment.
China's broader electric two-wheeler output, including e-bikes and light mopeds, runs to tens
of millions of units on a different statistical basis. The two are quoted interchangeably in
trade media and they are not the same market. **Which one a figure describes decides whether a
manufacturer is even relevant to this project.**

## Why there is no list of 5,000

The top ten hold 94% of the market. The remaining 6% is a long tail of small workshops and
rebadgers with no published volumes, no export operation and nothing verifiable. A 5,000-row
list can be generated from B2B storefront listings, but those entries are self-declared trading
companies — **authoritative-looking noise**, which is precisely what this project's sourcing
rules exist to exclude.

## Tiered for a first-time Pakistani entrant

Filtered on whether a deal is *plausible*, not on how impressive the manufacturer is.

- **Likely closed — Yadea.** Market leader, already partnered with Road Prince in Pakistan.
  Worth one confirming question, not a strategy. *(K3 flag.)*
- **Wrong segment — CFMOTO / ZEEHO.** Premium performance, nowhere near a 70cc replacement.
- **Worth asking — Luyuan, Zongshen, Xinri, Loncin, Tailing.** Credible scale; Zongshen and
  Loncin already export into markets like Pakistan. Whether any will appoint a first-time buyer
  at PKR 50m scale is answerable only by asking.
- **Most plausible — Jiangsu Huaihai and the ODM tier.** Huaihai is top-five *and* builds under
  other badges. Below it, ODM houses advertising openly (Wuxi MDKA, Wuxi Legend, Zhejiang Taiya)
  quote CKD/SKD kits and deal below container scale.

🔴 **But read `03-pakistan/nev-policy-2025-30.md` before acting on that last line.** Pakistan
already holds ~2 million units/year of electric two- and three-wheeler assembly capacity that
the government's own policy calls "heavily underutilized". The CKD-assembly route is therefore
*not* the obvious answer it appears to be here — capacity is not the scarce input.
