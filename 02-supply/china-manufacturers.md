# Chinese electric two-wheeler manufacturers — the list, ranked

**Recorded 26-Aug-2026.** Scope: electric two-wheelers. Not cars.

## 🔴 Read this before comparing any two numbers

**There are two different Chinese markets, and trade media quote them interchangeably.**

| Market | Scale | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Licence-plated electric motorcycles** | **3,506,200 units** (2025) | Registered vehicles. The segment a Pakistani commuter product sits in |
| **Total electric two-wheelers** | **32.33m units in H1 2025 alone** (+29.5% YoY) | Includes e-bikes and light mopeds. Installed base ~380m |

A company can be huge in one and absent from the other. **Yadea leads both; Aima leads the
broad market but does not appear in the licence-plated top ten.** Any ranking that mixes them
is meaningless.

**Export context:** China exported **22m+ electric two-wheelers worth US$5.8bn in 2024**, with
Europe, Asia and North America taking ~90% of export value.

## 🔴 Naming traps — how a list like this silently inflates

Every one of these has produced duplicate rows in published "top 10" lists:

- **Xinri = Sunra** — 新日. One company, two romanisations.
- **Tailing ≈ Tailg** — 台铃. Almost certainly one group; confirm before listing separately.
- **Aima / "Emma"** — 爱玛. Both are renderings of the same name.
- **CFMOTO / ZEEHO** — ZEEHO is CFMOTO's electric brand, not a separate maker.
- **Segway / Ninebot** — one company since the 2015 acquisition.

**Count the manufacturing entity, not the badge.** This is the single mechanism by which a list
reaches an impressive length without containing that many companies.

## Why there is no list of thousands

The top ten hold **94%** of the licence-plated market. The tail is small workshops and
rebadgers with no published volumes, no export operation and nothing verifiable. A very long
list can be generated from B2B storefronts, but those rows are self-declared trading companies
— authoritative-looking noise, which this project's sourcing rules exist to exclude.
