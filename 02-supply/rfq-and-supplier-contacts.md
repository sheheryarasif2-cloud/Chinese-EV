## The enquiry — kit suppliers

> **Subject:** CKD/SKD quotation request — electric two-wheeler for assembly in Pakistan
>
> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am evaluating the local assembly of electric two-wheelers in Karachi, Pakistan, and am at the
> stage of selecting supply partners. I found your CKD/SKD listings and would like a formal
> quotation.
>
> To be direct about where we are: this is an evaluation, not a repeat order. I am comparing
> several suppliers on total landed cost and on after-sales support, and I would rather have an
> accurate quotation than an optimistic one.
>
> Could you please quote on the following:
>
> **1. Exactly what the kit contains** — an itemised packing list, quoted **both with and without
> the battery**, since the battery appears to be the larger cost.
> **2. Battery** — chemistry, voltage, Ah, kWh, cycle rating at a stated depth of discharge, and
> price as a **separate line**.
> **3. FOB price in USD or CNY at 50, 100 and 200 units**, naming the port.
> **4. Whether you supply below full container load**, and the freight cost either way.
> **5. Crated dimensions and gross weight per unit** — I need to calculate units per 20ft container.
> **6. Warranty** — duration, what is covered, and specifically **who honours a claim made from
> Pakistan**.
> **7. Certification** — what you hold, and what documentation ships with the goods. Pakistan
> requires Engineering Development Board certification for the concessional duty rate, and a PSQCA
> release certificate at import.
> **8. Spare parts** — availability, pricing and lead time, and whether a starter parts package is
> offered with a first order.
> **9. Lead time** from deposit, payment terms, and **whether a letter of credit is acceptable**.
>
> Two further questions that matter for our market:
>
> - Pakistan's duty concession covers **"batteries other than lead acid"** only. **Lead-acid and
>   graphene packs are excluded** and are taxed at roughly double. Please quote lithium.
> - Our duty cycle is **80–128 km per day** for delivery riders. If you have a model built for that
>   rather than for private commuting, I would like to see it.
>
> Thank you — I will respond promptly to any questions.
>
> Sheheryar Asif
> Karachi, Pakistan

## The enquiry — battery suppliers

Same opening; items **3, 4, 5, 6, 9** identical, replacing 1–2 and 7–8 with:

> **1. Pack specification** — chemistry, voltage, Ah, kWh, cells used and their manufacturer,
> BMS specification, IP rating, weight and dimensions.
> **2. Cycle life at a stated depth of discharge**, and expected calendar life **at Karachi ambient
> temperatures — 40 °C+ for much of the year.** This is the single figure that decides our
> ownership cost, so please state the test condition rather than a headline number.
> **7. Certification** — UN38.3, CE, UL, IEC as applicable, and the **dangerous-goods documentation
> that accompanies a sea shipment.**
> **8. Whether you supply cells only**, and at what price — Pakistan admits **lithium-ion cells at
> 0% customs duty** for a registered battery manufacturer, so cell-level supply may suit us better
> than finished packs.

🔴 **That last point is the one worth watching.** If cell-level supply is available at a sensible
price, `fifth-schedule-primary-text.md` Route B applies — **0% duty, and no EDB vehicle
certification.** It could change the shape of the entry.

## What a quote can settle that desk research cannot

| Gate | What the quote answers |
|---|---|
| **K3 — will anyone supply at this scale** | Whether 50–200 units is a real order to them, or a nuisance |
| **K4 — can an LC be opened** | Whether they accept an LC at all, or demand T/T in advance |
| **K5 — battery warranty backstop** | **Who honours a claim from Pakistan.** The weakest point in the whole thesis |
| **K6 — landed cost** | Crated volume and weight, the last missing input to freight per unit |
| **K8 — parts and service** | Whether a starter parts package exists |
