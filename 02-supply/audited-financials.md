## 🔴 Finding 1 — Yadea's business is batteries as much as vehicles

From the audited FY2025 revenue table:

| Product | Revenue RMB'000 | % of total | Volume '000 units |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Electric bicycles | 16,208,695 | 43.8% | 11,453.5 |
| Electric scooters | 9,045,907 | 24.4% | 4,815.7 |
| *Vehicles subtotal* | *25,254,602* | *68.2%* | *16,269.2* |
| **Batteries and chargers** | **10,523,089** | **28.4%** | **Batteries 18,275.1 · Chargers 17,005.8** |
| E-2W parts | 1,230,545 | 3.4% | — |
| **Total** | **37,008,236** | 100.0% | 51,550.1 |

**Yadea sold 18.3 million batteries against 16.3 million vehicles.** Batteries and chargers are
**28.4% of revenue — RMB 10.5 billion**, larger than most competitors' entire business.

**Why this matters more than any ranking:** the recurring revenue in this industry is in
batteries, not vehicles. It also reframes **K5** — the market leader *monetises* battery
replacement rather than absorbing it as warranty cost. Luyuan's accounts show the same shape:
a separate line for "vehicle parts, such as tires and batteries, sold separately to distributors
for their provision of after-sales services."

## 🔴 Finding 2 — only one of the three actually makes money

| | **Yadea** FY2025 | **NIU** FY2025 | **Luyuan** FY2024 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | **RMB 37.01bn** (+31.1%) | — | RMB 5.07bn (flat) |
| Gross margin | **19.11%** (15.19% in 2024) | **19.6%** (15.2% in 2024) | **13.1%** (13.4% in 2023) |
| Bottom line | **+RMB 2.91bn** | 🔴 **−RMB 39.4m (loss)** | +RMB 116.1m |
| Net margin | **7.87%** | negative | **2.29%** |
| Units | 16.27m vehicles | 1.19m | — |

- **Yadea:** profit attributable rose **128.8%**, from RMB 1,272,374k to RMB 2,911,585k. Gross
  margin expanded from 15.19% to 19.11%. This is real operating leverage at scale.
- **NIU has lost money three years running** — RMB 271.8m (2023), RMB 193.2m (2024), RMB 39.4m
  / US$5.6m (2025). Narrowing, but still a loss **at essentially the same gross margin as
  Yadea.** Scale, not pricing, is what separates them.
- **Luyuan runs on 2–3% net margin.** Five-year audited revenue: RMB 2.38bn (2020) → 3.42bn →
  4.78bn → 5.08bn → **5.07bn (2024)** — growth stalled in 2024, and profit fell from RMB 145.6m
  to RMB 116.1m. FY2025 recovered to RMB 5.91bn revenue and RMB 175.1m profit (+50.9%), still
  under 3% net.

**Consequence for a partner search:** only Yadea has the balance sheet to fund a distant
distributor's warranty claims, marketing or credit terms — and its Pakistan territory is
already taken. **A thin-margin supplier cannot subsidise your market entry**, which bears
directly on K5's demand for an enforceable battery backstop.

## 🔴 Finding 3 — "international expansion" is far smaller than it sounds

This is where the filings most sharply contradict the press.

**NIU** — audited 20-F: *"We sold **1,192,039 units in 2025**, including 1,112,021 units sold in
the China market and **80,018 units sold in international markets**."* It sells through partners
in **more than 40 countries**.

**That is 6.7% of volume, averaging roughly 2,000 units per country per year.** A NASDAQ-listed
premium brand, four decades of partners, and international is a rounding error.

**Luyuan** — audited FY2024 annual report, on IPO proceeds: *"expanding the Group's sales in
international markets had been **delayed and unutilized**... approximately **HKD 6.1 million** as
of December 31, 2024."* The stated reason: *"in response to the current market uncertainties and
business environment, the Company has been cautious."*

**Luyuan's entire earmarked international-expansion budget was about HKD 6.1m — and it had not
spent it.** Press describes Luyuan as "expanding into international markets." The audited
accounts show a trivial budget, unspent, deliberately deferred.

⚠️ **Calibrate expectations accordingly.** If a listed Chinese maker moves ~2,000 units per
foreign market per year, then a Pakistani distributorship is a **small-volume business**,
whatever the size of the manufacturer behind it. That is a direct input to **K9**.

## Finding 4 — Yadea's Vietnam plant is operational, and export-facing

From the audited report: *"In early 2026, Yadea officially inaugurated its **US$100 million**
smart manufacturing plant in **Băc Ninh, Vietnam**... initial annual production capacity of **one
million units** and will serve as a key manufacturing hub supporting both **local demand and
exports**."*

Named growth markets: **Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines.** 🔴 **Pakistan is not named** in
Yadea's stated Southeast Asia strategy.

## Product-mix detail

**Yadea:** 70% of vehicle volume is electric *bicycles* (11.45m of 16.27m). Its 921,100
licence-plated motorcycles are **5.6% of total units** — the league table everyone quotes
measures a sliver of the business.

**Luyuan FY2024:** electric bicycles RMB 3,016.5m (**+11.7%**); electric scooters RMB 711.4m
(**−30.3%**); batteries sold with vehicles RMB 1,045.9m (−4.4%); parts sold separately to
distributors for after-sales **+15.7%**. Its scooter line contracted sharply while bicycles grew
— *"a structural change in market demand."* Brand history ~28 years; positioning is
liquid-cooled durability, *"A Ten-Year Ride."*

**NIU:** accessories and spare parts are 7.5% / 7.4% / **6.9%** of net revenues (2023/24/25) —
described in the filing as carrying higher gross margins.
