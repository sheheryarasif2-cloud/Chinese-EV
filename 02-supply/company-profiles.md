# What each company is actually doing

**Recorded 26-Aug-2026.** Company-level detail: strategy, scale, products, plants, and — the
part that decides everything — **the terms on which they will sell to you.**

`china-manufacturers.md` ranks them. This says what they are.

---

## 🔴 The finding that reorders the whole shortlist: the MOQ chasm

| Tier | Minimum order | What that costs at ~US$350 FOB |
|---|---|---|
| **Huaihai** (top-5 maker, own OEM/ODM arm) | **10,000 units/year** | ~US$3.5m |
| **Alibaba / trading-house tier** | **50–200 units** (some 1–5) | ~US$18k–70k |

**There is nothing in between.** The majors quote annual commitments roughly **twenty times the
entire PKR 50m capital ceiling**. The tier that will actually deal at 50–200 units is trading
houses and small ODMs — precisely where the **battery warranty backstop (K5) is weakest and
hardest to enforce from Pakistan.**

That is the real commercial shape of this market, and it was invisible from any ranking:
**scale and reachability are inversely related, and the gap between them is where a first-time
entrant falls.**

Trading-tier logistics, for reference: production 15–25 days after deposit, sea freight 25–35
days.

## Yadea 雅迪 — 1585.HK

**What it is doing: converting Chinese domestic dominance into a Southeast Asian manufacturing
footprint, and moving up-market on battery chemistry.**

| | |
|---|---|
| 2025 revenue | **RMB 37.0bn**, +31.1% (RMB 28.2bn in 2024) |
| Net profit attributable | **RMB 2.91bn**, **+128.8%** |
| Gross profit | RMB 7.07bn (RMB 4.29bn in 2024) |
| Total e-2W units 2025 | **16.3 million** |
| Base | Wuxi. Seven domestic plants. Exports to 100+ countries |

- **Vietnam: a US$100m factory with 1m units/year capacity**, on top of a base held since 2019.
  Indonesia R&D since 2024. Southeast Asia is explicitly the growth engine, driven by fuel
  prices pushing riders off petrol.
- Product ladder runs from entry commuter scooters to **sodium-ion battery** smart models —
  a chemistry bet that sidesteps lithium cost and cold-weather limits.
- 🔴 **Perspective on the ranking:** Yadea's 921,100 licence-plated motorcycles are only
  **5.6% of its 16.3m total units.** The league table everyone quotes measures a sliver of what
  these companies actually do.
- **Pakistan: taken.** Partnered with Road Prince; T5 around PKR 245,000.

**Verdict:** doubling profit and building regional capacity. Not looking for a small Pakistani
partner, and its territory is spoken for.

## Huaihai Holding Group 淮海 — the one that changes the picture

**What it is doing: operating as a multi-brand group with an existing overseas base network —
including Pakistan.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Founded | 1976 |
| Brands | **Huaihai, Zongshen, Hoann** |
| Structure | 27 wholly-owned subsidiaries |
| Scale | Total assets / business scale **> RMB 10bn**; Top 500 Chinese Private Enterprises |
| Plants | Xuzhou, Chongqing + 6 electric-scooter bases (Zhejiang, Guangdong, Jiangsu, Tianjin) |
| Reach | Marketing network across **96 countries** |
| 🔴 Overseas bases | **PAKISTAN**, India, Chile, Peru, Indonesia |
| **OEM/ODM MOQ** | **10,000 units/year** |

- Product span is wide: electric passenger tricycles, e-scooters, e-motorcycles, e-bikes,
  logistics and cold-chain vehicles. Service centres in Europe.
- 🔴 **It already has a Pakistan base.** That cuts both ways — it knows the market and the
  regulatory path, but it may prefer to expand its own operation over appointing anyone.

### ⚠️ Zongshen and Huaihai are entangled — the ranking may double-count

- **Jiangsu Zongshen Vehicle Industry Co.** was jointly established in **2003 by Zongshen
  Industrial Group and Huaihai Holding Group.**
- **Jiangsu Zongshen Automobile Industry Co.** (founded 1996) is a **Huaihai subsidiary.**

So #3 (Zongshen, 465,100) and #4 (Huaihai, 344,900) in the licence-plated table may be one
commercial group. Combined that is **810,000 units, 23.1%** — close to Yadea. **Confirm which
legal entity the association counted before treating them as separate targets.** Note that
Zongshen Industrial Group of Chongqing is itself a large separate entity; do not collapse them
without evidence.

**Verdict:** the most interesting name on the list, and the MOQ disqualifies a PKR 50m buyer
from its OEM programme. The question worth asking is not "will you supply me" but **"what are
you already doing in Pakistan."**

## Luyuan 绿源 — 2451.HK

**What it is doing: competing on engineering durability rather than price.**

- Revenue **~RMB 5.91bn**; net profit up **50%+**. Fifth largest in China's e-2W market by
  total revenue.
- Differentiator is **proprietary liquid-cooled motor technology**, marketed on motor lifespan
  — a genuine engineering claim rather than a styling one.
- Vast domestic distribution; international expansion stated but not yet built out like Yadea's.

**Verdict:** credible, listed, and earlier in its export journey than Yadea — which may make it
more approachable. Durability positioning suits Pakistani road conditions. Worth asking.

## Aima 爱玛 — 603529.SH

**What it is doing: aggressive capacity expansion plus a regional manufacturing build-out.**

- Strong cost control and profit growth; expanding production hard.
- **Vietnam plant in production; Indonesia site commissioning.** Following the same regional
  path as Yadea, one step behind.
- Leads the *broad* e-2W market but is **absent from the licence-plated top ten** — a different
  product mix, weighted to e-bikes and mopeds.

**Verdict:** regionalising now. Either receptive to a South Asia conversation, or already has
its own plans. Worth asking early, before the plans harden.

## What is driving all of them offshore

This is why they are receptive to export conversations at all:

- **A new national standard took effect November 2024**, regulating batteries and chargers.
  Combined with a 2025 trade-in policy, it is forcing a **replacement peak** in China.
- **Lead-acid is being pushed out.** Lithium e-bikes went from **4.62m units (2024) to ~5.44m
  (2025)** as safety rules bite.
- Domestic volume is enormous but maturing: **49.6m e-bikes sold in China in 2024**, ~54m
  expected 2025, against a stock **over 400 million**.
- **Battery swapping is scaling** — US$370m (2024) → US$460m (2025) → US$1.25bn by 2031, and
  **73.85% lithium**.

🔴 **Two consequences for Pakistan.** First, the cheap lead-acid machines being regulated out of
China are exactly what an emerging market absorbs — expect them to be offered, and expect the
warranty exposure that comes with them. Second, **battery swapping is where the Chinese industry
is actually investing**, and Pakistan's NEV Policy explicitly plans swapping stations for two-
and three-wheelers. That is a business adjacent to vehicles that does not require competing
with 2m units of idle assembly capacity.
