# China — manufacturers, models and prices

**Recorded 26-Aug-2026.** Prices **CNY**, retail in China unless marked ex-works.

⚠️ **Coverage note.** China has hundreds of manufacturers and thousands of SKUs; several majors
sell dozens of variants each. This covers the **significant manufacturers and representative
model tiers**, not every SKU — that list does not exist in any verifiable form.

Company financials and volumes are in `02-supply/audited-financials.md`.

### 🔴 The sodium-ion move is strategically important

Yadea's sodium packs claim **92% capacity at −20°C** and **80% charge in 15 minutes**. The
cold-weather case is irrelevant to Pakistan — **but the fast-charge and the lithium-free bill of
materials are not.**

Sodium sidesteps lithium price volatility and supply concentration entirely, at an entry price of
**CNY 3,299**. If that chemistry reaches export markets it changes the cost floor for everyone —
and it is the market leader, not a startup, deploying it.

**Watch this.** It is the single most consequential product development found in this research.

## Sunra / Xinri 新日 — same company, two romanisations

**Kemper** and **Keenes** premium electric motorcycles — **100–160 km/h**, **10 minutes to 80%
charge**. Genuine motorcycle-class performance rather than scooters.

Audited: revenue RMB 4.11bn FY2025, **1.23% net margin**, 2,284,597 units, **exports 2.48% of
revenue at 9.95% gross margin** — export is its least profitable channel.

## Aima 爱玛
Founded 1999, Tianjin. Audited FY2025: **RMB 25.10bn revenue, 8.11% net margin, 11,639,817 units**
— of which **2,615,982 electric two-wheeled motorcycles**. Model-level pricing not published in
accessible sources. **Vietnam plant operating, Indonesia commissioning.** Overseas revenue
**0.86% and falling**.

## Luyuan 绿源
Audited FY2024: RMB 5.07bn, 2.29% net margin. Brand ~28 years. Differentiator is
**proprietary liquid-cooled motor technology**, marketed as *"A Ten-Year Ride"* — a durability
claim that suits Pakistani road and heat conditions better than most.

## NIU 小牛 (NASDAQ)
Premium connected scooters. Audited FY2025: **1,192,039 units, of which only 80,018
international**; **net loss RMB 39.4m** — three consecutive loss-making years.

## The ODM / trading tier — where a small buyer actually transacts

**Wuxi MDKA New Energy · Wuxi Legend International · Zhejiang Taiya** and the wider B2B
storefront layer.

| | |
|---|---|
| **MOQ** | **50–200 units** typical; some quote 1–5 |
| **CKD/SKD** | Openly offered |
| **Lead time** | 15–25 days after deposit |
| **Sea freight** | 25–35 days |

⚠️ **Self-declared listings — a contact register, never evidence of scale or quality.**

🔴 **The chasm:** Huaihai wants 10,000 units a year; this tier will do 50. **Nothing sits in
between**, and the reachable tier is where a battery warranty backstop is weakest.
